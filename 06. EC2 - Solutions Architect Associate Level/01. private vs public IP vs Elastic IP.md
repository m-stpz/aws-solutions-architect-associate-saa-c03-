# IPs: public, private, elastic

- Networking has two sorts of IPs: v4 and v6
  - IPv4: 1.123.12.321
  - IPv6: 199:4545:3:200:f8ff:fe21:g7cf

- IPv4: most commonly used
  - allows for 3.7 billion different addreses
  - format: `[0-255].[0-255].[0-255].[0-255]`
- IPv6: newer, most commonly used of IoT
