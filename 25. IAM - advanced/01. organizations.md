# Organizations

- Global service
- allows to manage multiple AWS accounts
- Main account is the management account
  - other accounts are member accounts
- member accounts can only be part of one organization
- consolidated billing across all accounts
  - single payment method
- pricing benefits from aggregated usage
  - volume discount for EC2, S3...
- Shared reserved instances and savings plans discounts across accounts
- API's available to automate AWS account creation

## Core components

- Organization: top-level container holding all linked AWS accounts
- Management account: single AWS account that creates the organization
  - billing and governance
- Member accounts
  - all other AWS accounts inside the org
- Organizational units [OU]: logical folders used to group accounts with similar governance or workloads requirements
